- Normal product changes originate in `SemperSupra/garm-provider-truenas-private` and cross via constructive allowlisted projection.

- Pin third-party Actions by full commit SHA.
- Reject arbitrary images, mounts, Compose, resource escalation, or unknown execution profiles.
- Destructive lifecycle actions fail closed unless controller ownership and safe retirement are established.
- Successful public CI is evidence, not private promotion approval.
